How to review it

  1. Open Start the Professional preview above.
  2. Enter a test practice name, or leave the fields blank. Continue to the gift planner.
  3. Create a small fictional gift with one or more future release dates. Approve the preview plan.
  4. At Add Funds, inspect the schedule and the layout. Do not send funds. The regtest transaction construction needs a separate disposable Jade wallet and local Bitcoin Core adapter.
  5. Open Jade Plus to review the signing layout and camera placement. Without a valid disposable regtest handoff, signing remains locked by design.
  6. Open Finish to review the schedule, print layout and pending status. A verified transaction ID only appears after the developer regtest QR verification succeeds.

What remains before real customer use

  • Authenticated Jade wallet identity and a trustworthy public-wallet import.
  • Real funding address/payment QR with verified on-chain deposits and reliable fee calculation.
  • Independent approval of exact gift outputs, fees and recovery arrangements before hardware signing.
  • Authorized broadcast through the bc1q21 node and confirmation tracking.
  • A recipient recovery and maturity-release package that remains usable independently of bc1q21.
  • Full browser acceptance testing and production security review.

Important: A successful automated test or hardware regtest experiment does not by itself establish that these customer-facing functions are ready for mainnet.
