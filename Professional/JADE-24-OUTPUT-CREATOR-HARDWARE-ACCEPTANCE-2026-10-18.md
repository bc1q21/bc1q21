# bc1q21 Professional — Jade Plus 24-output creator signing acceptance

**Test date:** 2026-10-18  
**Branch:** `professional/isolated-release-prototype`  
**Result:** **PASS — creator hardware signing and local signature verification**, limited to the disposable offline test described below.

## Test purpose

Establish whether one Jade Plus animated-QR signing session can authorize **one Bitcoin funding PSBT containing 24 distinct P2SH/CLTV gift outputs**, with a separate creator change output, and whether the signed response can be independently checked offline. This is **not** a test of signing 24 separate PSBTs in one scan.

## Observed hardware evidence (owner-operated)

1. Opened the locally saved `jade-creator-24-offline.html` fixture, which identified the test as **disposable regtest only**, unsigned and not for real Bitcoin. It displayed **13 animated BC-UR QR frames**.
2. Jade Plus scanned the animated QR sequence and advanced to its output review. The hardware displayed **Output 1/25** through **Output 25/25**. The owner reported the first two outputs as **12,000** and **13,000 satoshis**, and that the 24 gift outputs had distinct addresses beginning with `3`, increasing by **1,000 satoshis** per release.
3. Output 25 displayed **0.00433500 BTC** (**433,500 satoshis**) as creator change. Jade showed **0.00002500 BTC** (**2,500 satoshis**) total fee.
4. The owner approved the transaction **once** after reviewing the outputs. Jade displayed its **Scan with wallet app** animated signed-transaction QR response.
5. The local webcam capture page reported **at least 17 frames captured**. The owner saved the response as `bc1q21_Jade_Signed_PSBT_QR_frames (6).txt`. The Jade Plus was subsequently switched off.
6. The final corrected standalone verifier was generated by GitHub Actions run **39149412574**, with successful packaging. Earlier verifier versions had JavaScript initialization failures and are **not** accepted as verification evidence.
7. The owner opened the final offline HTML verifier in Brave, selected the saved `(6).txt` capture, and supplied a screenshot showing the exact result:

> **PASS — Signature valid; 24 gifts, change and 2500 satoshi fee match. No broadcast.**

The PASS text is the **local verifier's reported result as shown in the screenshot**. The screenshot and the owner's hardware observations are the acceptance evidence; the signed capture bytes were **not independently ingested into this GitHub record**, and no claim of a separate chain-acceptance run is made for this creator transaction.

## Scope and engineering implications

**Established:** A **single funding transaction** can create 24 distinct future CLTV gift outputs, and Jade Plus can review and sign that funding transaction using an air-gapped animated QR roundtrip. The physical test involved **25 output review screens and one final approval**; do not market it as a single-tap or zero-review operation.

**Not established:** End-to-end product integration, broadcasting this specific signed creator transaction to Bitcoin Core, a multi-PSBT batch-signing feature, a secure private-schedule unlock via Jade, or a production security certification. The separate earlier four-release recipient CLTV tests remain valid evidence for **individual release signing** but are not an integrated lifecycle.
