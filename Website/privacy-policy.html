Privacy Policy

Smart Contract Giving, powered by bc1q21.com

Effective Date: May 21, 2026

Welcome to bc1q21.com (“bc1q21,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit or use Smart Contract Giving, powered by bc1q21.com.

bc1q21 was designed with a privacy-first philosophy and a non-custodial architecture intended to minimize centralized data collection whenever reasonably possible.

By using this website, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview of the bc1q21 Model

bc1q21 is a non-custodial Bitcoin gifting platform built directly on the Bitcoin blockchain.

The platform allows users to create Bitcoin time-locked gifts and future Bitcoin distributions using browser-based tools operating client-side within the user’s own device and browser environment.

bc1q21 does not function as a bank, custodial wallet, money transmitter, financial institution, or investment advisor.

Users remain solely responsible for wallet usage, seed phrase security, reviewing and confirming gift and transaction details before proceeding, funding Bitcoin gifts using the addresses or QR codes provided, and the gift settings and release conditions they choose.

2. Information We Collect

bc1q21 is intentionally structured to minimize the collection of personal information.

Depending on how you interact with the website, limited information may be received or generated.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact bc1q21 through email or a contact form, you may voluntarily provide your name, email address, message contents, and any additional information you choose to include.

This information is used solely for communication, support, operational, or business purposes.

If a user contacts bc1q21 through email or a contact form, the message is forwarded directly to bc1q21 by email.

These communications are not stored in a bc1q21 website database, though they may be retained within normal email records for communication, support, operational, or business purposes.

B. Technical and Server Information

Like most websites, bc1q21’s hosting server may automatically generate limited technical logs when visitors access the site.

These logs may include limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, basic server error information, and security-related information.

bc1q21 does not use these logs to create user profiles, track individual browsing behavior, collect personal financial information, or store seed phrases or private keys.

Any technical logs that may exist are used solely for basic website operation, troubleshooting, server stability, and security purposes.

C. Blockchain Information

Bitcoin blockchain transactions are public by nature.

When users create or broadcast Bitcoin transactions using bc1q21 tools, transaction data becomes publicly visible on the Bitcoin blockchain, blockchain activity may be permanently accessible through public blockchain explorers, and bc1q21 does not control or alter Bitcoin’s public transparency model.

Users are solely responsible for understanding the public nature of blockchain transactions.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

bc1q21 is structured to operate without collecting or storing sensitive personal or financial information from users.

bc1q21 does not collect or store Bitcoin private keys, seed phrases, wallet recovery phrases, custodial wallet credentials, government identification documents, banking credentials, passport information, social insurance numbers, or payment card information.

The core gifting process operates client-side within the user’s browser environment.

Sensitive cryptographic information remains under the direct control of the user and is not stored by bc1q21.

4. Non-Custodial Architecture

bc1q21 does not take custody or control of user Bitcoin.

The platform operates as a software tool that assists users in interacting with Bitcoin blockchain functionality.

Users retain sole responsibility for safeguarding their seed phrases and wallet information. bc1q21 cannot recover lost seed phrases, reverse blockchain transactions, access locked funds, or modify completed Bitcoin time-lock conditions.

Loss of wallet credentials, seed phrases, or recovery information may result in permanent and irreversible loss of access to Bitcoin.

5. Cookies and Analytics

bc1q21 may use limited analytics tools, including Google Analytics, on its public marketing website to understand basic visitor activity and improve public-facing communication.

Google Analytics is not used on the bc1q21 application pages that handle gift creation, seed phrases, private keys, or other sensitive cryptographic activity.

The bc1q21 application has been hardened so that private-key-related pages do not contact third-party analytics services.

Basic technical logs may also be generated as part of normal server operation when visitors access the website.

These logs may include limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, basic server error information, and security-related information.

bc1q21 does not use analytics or technical logs to store seed phrases, store private keys, create custodial wallet records, collect personal financial information, or access or control user Bitcoin.

Users may configure their browsers to limit or disable cookies if they choose, though certain website or browser-based functionality may operate differently as a result.

6. Third-Party Services

bc1q21 may interact with or reference third-party services, including Bitcoin nodes, blockchain explorers, GitHub, social media platforms, embedded media providers, infrastructure providers, or hosting providers.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

bc1q21 is not responsible for the privacy practices or data handling procedures of external services.

Users should independently review the policies of any third-party services they choose to use.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to help protect the website and related systems from unauthorized access, misuse, or disruption.

However, no website, server, software platform, or internet transmission can ever be guaranteed completely secure.

Users acknowledge and accept the inherent risks associated with internet-connected systems and digital assets.

8. International Users

bc1q21 is operated from the Republic of El Salvador.

By accessing the website from another jurisdiction, users acknowledge that their information may be processed or handled in jurisdictions with different privacy laws than their home country.

Users are responsible for determining whether use of the platform is lawful within their jurisdiction.

9. Children’s Privacy

bc1q21 is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

If we become aware that personal information from a minor has been submitted, reasonable efforts will be made to remove such information.
